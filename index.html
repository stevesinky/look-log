Looking trials

Shows one image at a time on a plain background, with one thing changed per trial. A second adult watches the child’s face, not the screen, and presses keys. Nothing flashes or moves.

1. Images

Add faces the child knows well, faces they don’t know, and a few non-face objects. Tag each one. Crop to the face with a plain background if you can.

2. Setup

3. What to vary

Use a preset, or All/None on each group. Do the slow-motion group only after neurology confirms it’s fine for the child.

Keys during a run: Space first look · ←/→ side they looked to · then 0 none, 1 brief, 2 sustained or tracked, 3 look and smile, U not alert/invalid · Esc ends. Set the same distance and seating every session and keep voices quiet during trials.
